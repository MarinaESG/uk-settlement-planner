Know what the planner can tell you.

Records and arithmetic can help you prepare. They do not establish lawful residence, assess exceptions, or decide whether an application will succeed. Rules can change. Check the official guidance for your intended application date and obtain regulated advice where needed.

Purchase terms and data handling

The advertised prices are £5 for one offline download and £1 per month for hosted saving. Checkout must show the same GBP amount. Monthly billing renews until cancelled through Manage subscription; access continues while the subscription remains active. Export is available without an active subscription.

The download is supplied after payment and can be kept on your device. This release does not request early loss of cancellation rights. Statutory rights are unaffected. Contact the operator for cancellation, refund, correction or account deletion requests. Paid launch requires the operator’s complete business details, cancellation/refund process and privacy notice to be published and reviewed.

Hosted account email is processed by Supabase; payment details are processed by Stripe. The app stores your workspace and a payment customer reference in Supabase. Demo records stay in memory until you export them. The offline version stores records in your browser and offers backups. No analytics or advertising cookies are included. Personal documents are not uploaded. Production retention, lawful basis, processor agreements, support and deletion timeframes must be completed by the operator before accepting real users.
